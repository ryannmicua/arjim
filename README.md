# Arjim

Arjim is my digital assistant. It is the system I talk to about all my work.

The goal is to have one assistant manage my workstreams, bring me what needs attention, keep me informed, and make sure nothing slips while authoritative records remain in their workspaces.

See [VISION.md](VISION.md) for the product vision, desired outcomes, and conditions of trust.

Status: Product definition and planning. Nothing is implemented yet.
